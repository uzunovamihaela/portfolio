Grundfos - Book a technician

Creating an easy booking process for customers to enhance the relationship with the brand and reduce frustrations

Role & responsibilities

  • Project organisation & management

  • User research & analysis of findings into actionable design requirements

  • Developed user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes

  • Designed key interface screens, focusing on accessibility and ease of use

The challenge


The challenge is to create a more comfortable decision making and buying process for clients, while also supporting the salespersons who meets them in- store.



The problem


How can Grundfos provide its customers - homeowners, installers, and wholesalers - with a professional, efficient, and highly personalise support experience, especially at key moments in their journey?

The solution

The team developed a digital feature for the Grundfos website that allows homeowners to quickly find, select, and book the nearest certified installer. The app provides availability, booking functionality, and direct communication between customers and technicians. It also supports uploading photos, price estimation, and clear guidance for both in-warranty and out-of-warranty situations.

Usability testing

We have performed two rounds of testing – with a lo-fi and a hi-fi prototype. This was to ensure that the concept and its features bring value to the target audience. During the lo-fi testings, users suggested:

  • Having a Booking summary screen with a Confirmation button
  • A screen where users can input their personal contact information
  • More detailed information about each installer
  • Possibility to see what is a rough price estimation of the service
