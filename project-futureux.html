Føtexa

Føtexa is a shopping cart which revolutionises the way people do grocery shopping - with smart check out features Føtexa builds a loyal and trustworthy relationship with the customers

Role & responsibilities

  • Developed creative briefs, mood boards, and style tiles to guide the design process

  • Built high-fidelity prototypes in Figma, ensuring consistency with iPad OS guidelines

  • Developed a website presentation for the concept to present the features

  • Collected feedback, identified usability issues, and implemented design improvements after each testing

The challenge


The main challenges of the project during 2020 were concerns about users’ health due to long waiting times at checkout, crowded store environments and being infected with COVID. We had to create a better, innovative and easier shopping experience by building a loyal and trustworthy relationship with the consumers.




The problem


  • How might we help our client in their attempts to create a better, innovative and easier shopping experience by building a loyal and trustworthy relationship with the consumers?
  • How might we help the customers save their time, move smoothly, have a delightful and enjoyable shopping, and avoid physical contact?
  • How might can we help them find items easily and quickly?

The solution

Our solution presented a futuristic shopping cart designed and developed for Føtex as a client. The idea is that the Smart Cart offers personalised shopping routes - customers can import their shopping lists and get routes through the store, minimising time spent searching for items. Real-time item finder helps to locate products, suggests alternatives, and informs users about stock levels and estimated restocks. Recipe inspiration is integrated to help customers discover new ideas and add ingredients directly to their shopping list. The concept features accessible design such as dyslexia-friendly fonts, clear navigation, and multi-language support ensure inclusivity for a broad audience.

Usability testing

The testing with different users uncovered a lot valuable insights for future improvements such as:

  • Making the receipt in categorised order
  • The checkout button should stay disabled if you do not have any products in the cart
  • Adding more options to the filter on recipes and having the main categories on the screen
  • Having the option to save the recipe by email or QR code
